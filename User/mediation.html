Meditation & Yoga Practices

Scroll through each step and immerse yourself in practice.

Surya Namaskar (12 Steps)

Step 1: Pranamasana
Prayer pose – stand tall, palms joined.
Step 2: Hastauttanasana
Arms raised overhead, gentle backbend.
Step 3: Padahastasana
Bend forward, hands near feet.
Step 4: Ashwa Sanchalanasana
Step one leg back, look forward.
Step 5: Dandasana
Plank pose, body straight.
Step 6: Ashtanga Namaskara
Chest, knees, and chin touch floor.
Step 7: Bhujangasana
Cobra pose, chest lifted.
Step 8: Parvatasana
Mountain pose, inverted V.
Step 9: Ashwa Sanchalanasana
Other leg forward, look up.
Step 10: Padahastasana
Forward bend again.
Step 11: Hastauttanasana
Arms raised overhead again.
Step 12: Pranamasana
Return to prayer pose.

Mindful Breathing (4 Steps)

Step 1: Sit Comfortably
Straight spine, relaxed shoulders.
Step 2: Focus on Breath
Notice inhale and exhale.
Step 3: Count Breaths
Count silently up to 10, then restart.
Step 4: Return Awareness
Bring mind back when it wanders.

Body Scan Meditation (4 Steps)

Step 1: Feet
Notice sensations at the feet.
Step 2: Legs
Move awareness up through legs.
Step 3: Chest & Back
Feel breath moving the chest.
Step 4: Head & Face
Relax jaw, forehead, and eyes.

Chakra Meditation (7 Chakras)

Root Chakra
Grounding & stability.
Sacral Chakra
Creativity & emotions.
Solar Plexus Chakra
Confidence & power.
Heart Chakra
Love & compassion.
Throat Chakra
Expression & truth.
Third Eye Chakra
Intuition & clarity.
Crown Chakra
Spiritual connection.

Loving-Kindness / Metta (4 Stages)

Stage 1: Self
May I be happy and peaceful.
Stage 2: Loved Ones
May they be healthy and safe.
Stage 3: Neutral People
Wishing well to those we don’t know closely.
Stage 4: All Beings
Universal compassion and love.
