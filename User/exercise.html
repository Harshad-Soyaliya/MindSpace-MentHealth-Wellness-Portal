Exercise & Fitness Guide

Step-by-step routines for fat loss, muscle building, full body workouts, and stretching.

Fat Loss Exercises

Jumping Jacks (4 Steps)
Step 1: Start
Stand upright, arms at sides.
Step 2: Jump Out
Feet apart, arms overhead.
Step 3: Jump In
Feet together, arms down.
Step 4: Repeat
Continue rhythmically, steady breathing.
High Knees (3 Steps)
Step 1: Stand Tall
Arms at sides, feet hip-width apart.
Step 2: Drive Knees
Lift knees alternately to chest level.
Step 3: Pump Arms
Move arms naturally, increase speed.

Muscle Building

Push-Ups (4 Steps)
Step 1: Start
Hands shoulder-width, body straight.
Step 2: Lower Down
Chest moves toward floor.
Step 3: Hold
Pause briefly, keep core tight.
Step 4: Push Up
Return to start, arms extended.
Squats (4 Steps)
Step 1: Stand Tall
Feet shoulder-width apart.
Step 2: Lower Hips
Bend knees, hips back.
Step 3: Hold
Thighs parallel to floor.
Step 4: Rise Up
Push through heels, stand tall.

Full Body Workouts

Burpees (5 Steps)
Step 1: Stand
Feet shoulder-width apart.
Step 2: Squat Down
Hands on floor.
Step 3: Kick Back
Into push-up position.
Step 4: Push-Up
Optional for strength.
Step 5: Jump Up
Explosive jump, arms overhead.
Mountain Climbers (4 Steps)
Step 1: Start
High plank, hands under shoulders.
Step 2: Drive Knee
Bring one knee to chest.
Step 3: Switch Legs
Alternate knees quickly.
Step 4: Maintain Pace
Keep back flat, core tight.

Stretching & Flexibility

Forward Bend (3 Steps)
Step 1: Stand Tall
Feet hip-width apart.
Step 2: Bend Forward
Hinge from hips, reach down.
Step 3: Relax
Neck loose, hold 20–30 sec.
Shoulder Stretch (3 Steps)
Step 1: Extend Arm
Bring right arm across chest.
Step 2: Hold
Use left hand to press closer.
Step 3: Switch Sides
Repeat with left arm.

Healthy Dieting & Nutrition

Balanced Plate (4 Steps)
Step 1: Vegetables
Fill half your plate with colorful veggies.
Step 2: Protein
Add lean protein like beans, fish, or chicken.
Step 3: Whole Grains
Quarter of plate with brown rice or oats.
Step 4: Healthy Fats
Add nuts, seeds, or olive oil.
Hydration (3 Steps)
Step 1: Start Day
Drink 1 glass of water in the morning.
Step 2: Throughout Day
Keep sipping water regularly.
Step 3: Limit Sugary Drinks
Choose water or herbal teas instead.
Mindful Eating (4 Steps)
Step 1: Pause
Take a moment before eating.
Step 2: Eat Slowly
Chew thoroughly, enjoy flavors.
Step 3: Listen
Notice hunger and fullness signals.
Step 4: Gratitude
Appreciate the food you have.
Portion Control (3 Steps)
Step 1: Use Smaller Plates
Helps avoid overeating.
Step 2: Measure Portions
Check serving sizes.
Step 3: Stop at 80% Full
Leave some space in stomach.
